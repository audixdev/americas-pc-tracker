No.

AnyXboxX

Xbox Series emulator focused on this one game, built on VIctory

Leader

VIctory

Level

Boots, runs the menus at the game's own 30 fps and loads into the world in about 50 seconds. The first mission's title card renders, the scene after it stays dark, and each frame takes 0.5 to 1.8 seconds. Entering the world can hang a real graphics card.

Source: project README, tested on one RX 6700 XT. Entry changed 18:20 UTC, Tue 24 Nov. Last commit 09:53 UTC, Tue 24 Nov.

Time to playable

About 6½ days

Best guess: the afternoon of Tue 1 Dec.

Playable means: At least one project shows 30 minutes or more in the game world under player control, at 20 frames a second or better on some PC, without a crash.

What the estimate rests on

  • The executable surfaced on 23 November. Within about a day, three code bases reported the game world running.
  • The front-runners are in the world but blind: the picture is black or one flat colour, and lighting has not started.
  • Speed is the long pole. The best measured figure is 3.2 frames a second, up from 0.2 the same day. Playable needs about six times that.
  • Work is shared across projects. AnyXboxX builds on VIctory, and fixes move between XCL, VIctory and Palmwave in days, not weeks.
  • Takedowns are a real risk. Leonida and at least one community server are already gone.

Method and limits

Playable builds
None exist. Anything offered as a playable download of GTA VI for PC is fake, and fakes have carried malware.
What the projects use
They work from a leaked Xbox Series build. Rockstar has not announced a PC version. This page does not link to the leak or explain how to get or run it.
Sources
Each project's public repository: its README, status files, commits and pull requests. Press reports where a project has no public code. Every row names its source.
Claims
Stages are what each project says about itself, unless the source line says otherwise. This page does not run the code, and most claims are unverified.
Ranking
By stage first, then by how far within that stage the project's own description goes. An equals sign marks a tie.
Who writes it
An AI assistant (Claude) checks the repositories when asked and rewrites the entries. It can be wrong; the source links are there to check it.
Affiliation
None. Not connected to Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Microsoft or any project listed. Names link to repositories to credit the work.

The seven stages

  1. 1LoadsThe game's executable loads with its imports resolved.
  2. 2Starts upThe game's own start-up code runs: memory, threads, archives.
  3. 3Graphics deviceThe game creates its graphics device and queues on the PC.
  4. 4First framesFrames are presented to a window, even if they are black.
  5. 5Recognisable pictureThe game's own screens can be seen: logos, menus, video or setup pages.
  6. 6In-gameThe game world is loaded and running, with or without a usable picture.
  7. 7Playable30 minutes or more in the world under player control, at 20 frames a second or better, without a crash.
