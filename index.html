Is GTA VI playable on PC yet?

No.

Twenty public projects and two private builds have reached stage 6 of 7, in-game. No project is confirmed playable yet. Vice-Tay's README reports a median of 21.1 frames a second over a 30-minute session by hand on an RTX 4090, but one session, self-reported, and it does not say how the session ended. AnyXboxX reports a median of about 24 over a 14-minute session on an RTX 4070 SUPER, also self-reported. KLBF Lab reports an average of 14 over 37 minutes so far and 77 minutes on an earlier build; VIctory-7900XTX reports about 16 to 20 in short runs. Playable needs 20 for 30 minutes without a crash.

Best guess for playable: about 3½ days, on the afternoon of Mon 30 Nov.

29 projects are trying to run the Xbox Series build of GTA VI on a PC. This page reads their public repositories and reports how far each says it has got. Last checked 06:54 UTC on Fri 27 Nov.

Method and limits

Playable builds
None exist. Anything offered as a playable download of GTA VI for PC is fake, and fakes have carried malware.
What the projects use
They work from a leaked Xbox Series build. Rockstar has not announced a PC version. This page does not link to the leak or explain how to get or run it.
Sources
Each project's public repository: its README, status files, commits and pull requests. Press reports where a project has no public code. Every row names its source.
Claims
Stages are what each project says about itself, unless the source line says otherwise. This page does not run the code, and most claims are unverified.
Ranking
By stage first. In-game projects, at stage 6, are then ordered by their three lanes, explained below the stages. In the other stages, by the furthest point each project has reached in the game's own sequence of screens, furthest first; when two are at about the same point, the one with a measured frame rate, an unmodified build or a public, first-hand source ranks higher. An equals sign marks a tie that none of these separates.
Who writes it
An AI assistant (Claude) checks the repositories when asked and rewrites the entries. It can be wrong; the source links are there to check it.
Affiliation
None. Not connected to Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Microsoft or any project listed. Names link to repositories to credit the work.

The seven stages

  1. 1LoadsThe game's executable loads with its imports resolved.
  2. 2Starts upThe game's own start-up code runs: memory, threads, archives.
  3. 3Graphics deviceThe game creates its graphics device and queues on the PC.
  4. 4First framesFrames are presented to a window, even if they are black.
  5. 5Recognisable pictureThe game's own screens can be seen: logos, menus, video or setup pages.
  6. 6In-gameThe game world is loaded and running, with or without a usable picture.
  7. 7Playable30 minutes or more in the world under player control, at 20 frames a second or better, without a crash.

The three lanes

A project that is in-game, at stage 6, is also read on three lanes. Each step is this page's reading of what the project reports about itself, or of the source its row names, and an empty meter means no figure is reported. Blue marks the steps that meet the playable bar: 20 frames a second and 30 minutes without a crash.

Rows at that stage are ordered by their lanes and nothing else: fewest steps short of the bar on speed and stability together, then the better picture, the higher frame rate and the longer session.

What the project says is on screen in the game's world.

  2. 2Lit where the sun falls, shadows black or dark
  3. 3Lit in light and shade, with visible faults
  4. 4Lit and whole, small faults only
